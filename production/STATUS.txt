CAD
  pcbfirstdesign.step: KiCad export supplied on 2026-09-26.
  OxideKeys-review-assembly.step: PCB outline, component-size envelopes,
  base plate, switch deck, display flap and four illustrative spacers.
  OxideKeys-review-assembly-detailed.step.gz: the same assembly with the
  full available KiCad component geometry. The production workflow also
  expands it to OxideKeys-review-assembly-detailed.step.
  Plate outlines and cutouts come from
  enclosure/Oxidekeysenclosure.svg. The 3 mm sheet thickness, 6 mm spacer
  height, and 65 degree open flap position are assumptions, not measurements.
  The display module, hinge hardware, keycaps and fasteners are not modeled.
  Check physical clearance and laser kerf against actual parts before cutting.

FIRMWARE
  firmware/firmware-source.zip contains the editable Rust source, lockfile,
  linker memory file and Cargo target configuration. The production workflow
  builds oxide-keys.uf2 from the repo source and checks its UF2 block format.
  BUILD.txt records the exact source commit. Flashing on hardware is untested.

PCB
  gerbers.zip is copied without modification from hardware/gerbers/gerbers.zip.
